**Objective:** Create your autobotAI account with secure authentication and activate your subscription.

**Estimated Time:** 5 minutes

## 1.1 Signing Up

### Step 1: Visit the Sign-Up Page
Go to the following link:  
👉 [https://autobot.live/app#/start](https://autobot.live/app#/start)

### Step 2: Create a New Account
Click on **Create Account** and provide the following details:
- **Name**
- **Email** — *Only official email is allowed*
- **Phone Number**
- **Password** — *Must include at least one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, one number, one special character, and be more than 8 characters long.*

![Sign Up Page](../../static/sign-up.png)

### Step 3: Verify Email and Set Up MFA
1. Enter the **OTP** received on your registered email and submit.  
2. Once verified, you will be prompted to **set up MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)**.

Use an authenticator app such as **Zoho OneAuth**, **Authy**, or **Google Authenticator** to:
- Scan the displayed QR code.  
- Enter the generated verification code.  
- Click **Confirm** to complete the setup.

### Step 4: Close the Community Edition Popup

Once you've logged in successfully, a **Community Edition Features** popup may appear. Click the **close (✕)** icon in the top-right corner to dismiss it before continuing.

![Close the Community Edition Popup](../../static/community-edition-popup.png)

### Step 5: Activate Temporary Subscription Using a Coupon Code

1. After closing the popup, you will see the **Subscription Popup**.

![Subscription Popup](../../static/subscription-popup.png)

2. Click on **Apply Coupon Code**. You will see the following screen.

![Coupon Popup](../../static/coupon-code-popup.png)

3. Enter the 10-character coupon code (Uppercase letters) to activate your trial subscription.

**Coupon Code:** `AWSAISECWS`

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: OTP not received for email verification
**Solution:** Check spam folder. Wait 2-3 minutes and request a new OTP if needed.

### Issue: MFA QR code not scanning
**Solution:** Ensure your authenticator app has camera permissions. Try manually entering the setup key instead.

### Issue: Coupon code not working
**Solution:** Ensure you're entering the code in UPPERCASE: `AWSAISECWS`. Contact workshop staff if issues persist.
